Kids Champ Artwork Generator V4.8 Auto V4

1. Square artwork continues to use the active Square master; Portrait, Landscape
   and Square selections stay independent and persistent.
2. Smart placement now follows the measured admin workflow: after safe cleanup,
   the complete artwork is stretched independently in X/Y to the exact frame.
3. Smart no longer performs a second proportional Fill Frame crop, so important
   top, bottom and side drawing content is retained.
4. The light-sheet fallback now requires light/low-chroma paper plus a visibly
   different outside background. Bright sky, sand or coloured artwork bands are
   no longer accepted as a sheet merely because they form a large region.
5. Manual Full Artwork, Fill Frame and Photoshop-style Free Transform remain
   available for exceptional images.
6. PNG/TIFF alpha and the transparent video opening are unchanged.
